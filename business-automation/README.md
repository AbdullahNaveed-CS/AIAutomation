# End-to-End Business & Agency Operations Automation

Production n8n workflows automating lead qualification, client onboarding, and operational pipelines for service and renewable energy businesses.

## Included Workflows
* **`Automate entire solar company.json`:** Automates solar lead capture, site/load estimation routing, proposal generation, and customer follow-up sequences.
* **`Complete consultancy United States agency.json`:** Automates client inquiry triage, advisory intake questionnaires, CRM logging, and consultation scheduling.

## Setup
1. Import the `.json` file into n8n (**Workflows → Import from File**).
2. Configure your CRM/Google Sheets, Gmail/SMTP, and AI model API credentials.
